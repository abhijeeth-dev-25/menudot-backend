- Option enter there name and number of people. 

How qr works:
- When the  customer comes and the server will serve a table for them, then the server on qr by there table 
- Until the server on there qr option to scan, until now -> no customer can able can the scanner.
- It won’t show any items and don’t take orders.
- After completion bill the qr will automatically, offs the scanning permission.
- At a time only one user can able scan from the side of table.
- After scanning the qr restaurant will know the device name and time details of the particular user for security purpose.
